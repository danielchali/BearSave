MADE FOR HOW YOU WATCH

More room for
the good stuff.

Pick a video, press play, and make the experience fit your day.

01 / PLAY

Take playback with you

Keep audio playing in the background, or float a video with popup and picture-in-picture playback.

02 / SAVE

Keep a copy close

Download videos, audio, and subtitles for the moments when you want them on hand.

03 / ORGANIZE

Build your own library

Subscribe to channels, get new-content notifications, and shape local playlists, groups, and feeds.

04 / FIND

Search beyond titles

Look for videos, audio, channels, playlists, and comments across supported services.

YOUR APP, YOUR CHOICE

Private by design.
Open by nature.

Core functionality does not require an account or Google Play Services. BearTube does not collect data without your consent; network requests go directly to supported services or their public interfaces.
